Research line

Schizophrenia

Chloride cotransporter imbalance, hippocampal network dysfunction, and translational pharmacology.

We investigate how the same chloride cotransporter machinery that fails in epilepsy also contributes to the circuit dysfunction underlying schizophrenia. Using pharmacological and genetic rodent models, we study how altered NKCC1/KCC2 balance disrupts GABAergic inhibition in the hippocampus, contributing to the network-level abnormalities associated with the disorder.

This work has also driven translational efforts to repurpose bumetanide as a candidate antipsychotic, extending our chloride-cotransporter framework from basic mechanism toward potential treatment.

Model

Pharmacological and genetic rodent models of schizophrenia

Key targets

NKCC1 / KCC2 chloride cotransporter balance · Hippocampal GABAergic inhibition

Translational work

Bumetanide repurposed as a candidate antipsychotic
