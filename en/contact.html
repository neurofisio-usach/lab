Join the lab

Undergraduate students

If you are a student of Biochemistry, Biology or a related program at USACH and are interested in a thesis project on hippocampal electrophysiology or animal models of neuropsychiatric disorders, get in touch — we are happy to talk about open positions and available projects.

Graduate students (MSc/PhD)

We welcome inquiries from prospective graduate students interested in ion channel physiology, chloride cotransporters, GABAergic signaling, or circuit-level dysfunction in neurological and neuropsychiatric disorders. Please write directly to Dr. Rojas with a short description of your research interests and your CV.

Postdoctoral researchers

We are open to discussing postdoctoral opportunities with candidates trained in electrophysiology, neurophysiology or related fields. Please contact us with your CV and a short description of your research interests.
