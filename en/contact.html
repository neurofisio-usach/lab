Join the lab

Undergraduate students

If you're a Biochemistry, Biology, or related-program student at USACH interested in a thesis project involving hippocampal electrophysiology or animal models of neuropsychiatric disease, get in touch — we're happy to discuss current openings and potential projects.

Graduate students (Masters/PhD)

We welcome inquiries from prospective graduate students interested in ion channel physiology, chloride cotransporters, GABAergic signaling, or circuit-level dysfunction in neurological and neuropsychiatric disorders. Please email Dr. Rojas directly with a brief statement of your research interests and a CV.

Postdoctoral researchers

We are open to discussing postdoctoral opportunities for candidates with backgrounds in electrophysiology, neurophysiology, or related fields. Please reach out with a CV and brief description of research interests.
